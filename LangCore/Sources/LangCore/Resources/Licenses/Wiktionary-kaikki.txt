Spanish dictionary data

Definitions come from English Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), extracted into machine-readable form by
Tatu Ylonen's wiktextract and published by kaikki.org. Wiktionary content is available under the Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0) and the GNU Free Documentation Licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Authors: the Wiktionary contributors, see the page history of each entry on en.wiktionary.org.

Changes: the extract was filtered to the entries for Spanish words and converted to a SQLite database for fast
look-up, keeping each word's part of speech and condensed English definitions. The database in this app is an
adaptation of that data and is made available under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0. The scripts that build it are in
the project's Scripts/dictionaries folder: https://github.com/surreptus/aomame
