KANJIDIC2 (kanji readings and meanings)

This application uses the KANJIDIC2 kanji file. The file is the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and
Development Group, and is used in conformance with the Group's licence:
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

KANJIDIC2 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: the data was converted to a SQLite database for fast look-up. Each kanji keeps its on'yomi and kun'yomi
readings and its English meanings (the first six); name readings, other languages' meanings, stroke counts and index
numbers were left out. The database in this app is an adaptation of KANJIDIC2 and is made available under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 4.0. The script that builds it, Scripts/dictionaries/build_kanjidic.py, is in the project, so it can
be rebuilt from the original data: https://github.com/surreptus/kikimi
