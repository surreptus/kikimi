## Watch, collect, learn, watch again

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="1 · Add something you enjoy">
    Drop in a video from your Mac, with its subtitles if it has them. Without subtitles, Kikimi writes them out on your Mac.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2 · Click the words you meet">
    Every word from your videos is listed with how often you hear it and where, and each video shows how much of it you know.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3 · Learn them in context">
    Short daily lessons, then spaced-repetition reviews. Every card uses a sentence from your own videos. Then watch again and understand more.
  </Card>

## Made for comprehensible input

The idea is simple: you learn a language by understanding a lot of it. Each video shows how many of its words you know, so you can pick material that is mostly familiar with a little new. One button starts learning the commonest words that would make a video comfortable to follow. The figure counts words you know. It is a guide, not a promise that you will understand every line.

## Private by design

Your videos, transcripts and progress stay on your Mac. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Kikimi goes online only to download a speech model and, when you ask, to check for a new version. Your original files are only read, never changed or deleted. [The privacy policy](/kikimi/privacy/) says it in full.

## Finding videos to learn from

Kikimi never finds or downloads videos; it works on files you give it. If you have none, the guide covers [what makes good material and where to look for files you may keep](/kikimi/guide/finding-material/), including searches for each language on the Internet Archive.

## Questions

<details>
<summary>macOS says it can't verify Kikimi. What do I do?</summary>

Kikimi is a beta and is not notarized yet, which Apple charges a yearly fee for, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. The [download page](/kikimi/download/) shows the steps.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need subtitles?</summary>

No. Without them, Kikimi transcribes the audio on your Mac using a speech model you download once. With an `.srt` or `.vtt` file for the video, nothing is transcribed and it is the fastest way to start.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use videos from YouTube or Netflix?</summary>

Kikimi never downloads anything from video sites and does not work on streaming sites. It works on files you give it, so use videos you own or may copy and study.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work offline?</summary>

Yes, once a speech model is downloaded, or if you only import videos with subtitles.
</details>

More in the [common questions](/kikimi/faq/).
