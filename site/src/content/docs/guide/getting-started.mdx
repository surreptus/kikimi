---
title: Your first session
description: From an empty Kikimi to your first reviews.
---

The first time you open Kikimi it asks which languages you are learning. You can change that later in Settings (<kbd>⌘,</kbd>) and switch language from the sidebar.

## 1. Add a video

On **Videos**, drop in a video from your Mac, or press <kbd>⌘O</kbd>. Then press **Start import**.

- If you have a subtitle file for it (`.srt` or `.vtt`), press **Add subtitles** first. Nothing is transcribed, so you need no speech model, and you start sooner.
- If you don't, the first time Kikimi offers a one-time download of a speech model (about 1.5 GB, 1.6 GB for Japanese). It is saved on your Mac and nothing is uploaded.

## 2. Let it finish

Kikimi writes out what is said, with times. This is the long step when there are no subtitles. You can use other pages while it runs. See [importing videos](/kikimi/guide/importing-videos/) for each step.

## 3. See how much you know

When it is done, the video shows how much of its words you know, such as *62% of words known*. Until you play a new video, its row offers two next steps: **Learn** the commonest words that would make it easier to follow, or **Mark the words I know**. Learn opens lessons on those words, five at a time, where you can ignore names and mistakes or mark words you already know, so only the ones you are taught join your reviews. Mark the words I know shows the commonest new words, and you tick the ones you know. Only those are marked known (you can undo it).

## 4. Watch, and click words

Open the video. The subtitles are on the right. Point at a word, or move along a line with <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, to see what it means. Mark it **Learn** (<kbd>L</kbd>), **Known** (<kbd>K</kbd>) or **Ignore** (<kbd>X</kbd>). More in [the player](/kikimi/guide/player/).

## 5. Learn

On **Learn**, today's lessons teach you five new words at a time, then quiz you on them straight away. Words you start learning come back as reviews at set times, each with a sentence from your own videos. The number beside Learn in the sidebar is how many reviews are due. More in [lessons and reviews](/kikimi/guide/lessons-and-reviews/).

## 6. Watch again

Come back to Videos. Each video shows how much of it you now know, and **Next up** suggests the one that fits you best.

:::tip
The goal is not to finish cards. It is to understand more of what you watch.
:::
