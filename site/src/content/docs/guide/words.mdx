title: Words

**Words** lists every word found in your videos, for the language you have selected, ordered by how often you hear it. Press <kbd>⌘F</kbd> to search.

## Statuses

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **New** | Found in your videos, not started |
| **Learning** | In your reviews, moving up the stages |
| **Known** | You marked it known, so it is not reviewed |
| **Finished** | It went through every review stage and no longer comes back |
| **Ignored** | Set aside, such as a name or a mistake. Kept, but hidden, left out of lessons and of how much of a video you know |

The table also shows **Seen** (how many lines it appears in) and **Next review**. Filter by status or type of word from the header.

## Choosing words

- Select words and a bar appears under the list. It shows only the buttons that would change at least one selected word: **Reset** (back to new) for words that aren't new, **Ignore** to set them aside, **Known** for words you already know, and **Learn** for new words. With nothing selected the bar is hidden. A button changes only the selected words it applies to, so **Learn** on a mixed selection starts just the new ones.
- Every one of those buttons can be undone. A message appears at the top of the window with **Undo**, which puts each word back exactly as it was, with its stage and next review. It stays for about eight seconds.
- **Select** has shortcuts, such as the 25, 50, 100 or 250 most-seen new words.
- To mark a list of words you already know in one go, use **Settings > Data**: choose a text file of words, one per line, such as an Anki export.
- To take words to Anki, use **File > Export Words to Anki…** for the selected words, or for the ones you are learning. It makes a file Anki can import.

Marking words known makes each video's coverage truer, since coverage counts the words you know.

## The word panel

Select a word to open its panel:

- Its dictionary meaning. Japanese words show every reading, and each kanji its on'yomi and kun'yomi, JLPT level and school year. Spanish, French and German nouns show their article (el/la, le/la, der/die/das).
- Its status, and the same buttons as the bar, in the same order: **Restore** or **Ignore**, **Known** and **Learn**.
- Sentences from your own videos with the word in them. **Hear** plays the sentence, and the video's name and time open the video at that moment.
