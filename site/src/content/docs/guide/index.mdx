Kikimi helps you understand more of what you watch. You add videos from your Mac, it finds their words, and you learn the ones you want with sentences from those same videos.

Never used it? Start with [your first session](/kikimi/guide/getting-started/).

## Start here

- [Your first session](/kikimi/guide/getting-started/): from an empty app to your first reviews.
- [Importing videos](/kikimi/guide/importing-videos/): files, subtitles, speech models and what each step does.

## Using Kikimi

- [The player](/kikimi/guide/player/): clickable subtitles, looping a line, hiding subtitles and clips.
- [Words](/kikimi/guide/words/): the table of every word you have met, and how to choose what to learn.
- [Lessons and reviews](/kikimi/guide/lessons-and-reviews/): daily lessons, the review schedule, card types and practice.
- [Shadowing](/kikimi/guide/shadowing/): say a sentence aloud and compare yourself with the original.

## Looking after it

- [Backups](/kikimi/guide/backups/): save and restore your progress.
- [Troubleshooting](/kikimi/guide/troubleshooting/): when something doesn't work.

Something missing or wrong? [Open an issue](https://github.com/surreptus/kikimi/issues) (it is public, so leave out personal details).
