---
title: Backups
description: Save and restore your progress, and where your data lives.
---

## Save a backup

In **Settings > Data**, **Save backup** writes your words, stages and review dates to a `.kikimi` file. It is readable JSON, so it is not locked in.

Turn on **Include my videos and transcripts** to save a folder instead, so a new Mac needs no re-transcribing. Speech models are never included; they download again when needed.

## Restore

Choose the file or folder, or double-click it. You see what would change first, and **nothing you already have is overwritten**. Restoring adds what is missing.

## Where your data lives

Kikimi keeps its database, your compressed videos and its speech models in its own folder inside your Mac's Library. Your original video files are never touched. **Settings > Data** (and the Storage screen) shows what is using space, and lets you remove videos or models. Kikimi never permanently deletes your data itself: it shows you where it is and lets you.

## Starting fresh

To start again, save a backup first. Then in **Settings > Data**, under **Danger zone**, press **Start over…**. Kikimi moves your words, progress and videos into a **Set Aside** folder and quits, and opens empty the next time. Nothing is deleted, and the speech models stay, so there is nothing to download again.

Back in Settings > Data, **Show Set Aside Folder in Finder** shows where they went. To go back, quit Kikimi and move the items inside the dated folder (the database `kikimi.sqlite`, and the `Library` and `Staging` folders) back up into Kikimi's own folder. When you are sure you don't want them, delete the Set Aside folder yourself: Kikimi never does.
