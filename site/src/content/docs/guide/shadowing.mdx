The **Shadow** card practises speaking. You hear a short sentence from your own videos, say it aloud, and play yourself beside the original. Choose it on the Learn page: pick **Sentence**, then **Shadow** in the menu beside it.

## How it works

1. The front shows the meaning of the word. Press **Hear it** (<kbd>R</kbd>), then repeat the sentence aloud.
2. Press **Record** (<kbd>M</kbd>) and say it. Press **Stop** (<kbd>M</kbd>) when you finish. Recording also stops by itself after about twice the sentence's length plus 3 seconds, up to 20 seconds.
3. The card shows the sentence with the word picked out. **Hear it** plays the original, and **Hear yourself** (<kbd>Y</kbd>) plays your recording.
4. Decide for yourself: **Got it** (<kbd>→</kbd>) or **Missed it** (<kbd>←</kbd>).

Nothing judges your pronunciation. There is no score, because automatic grading of pronunciation isn't reliable, and you can hear the difference yourself. Only short sentences are used.

## The microphone

Kikimi asks for microphone access the first time you press Record, and never otherwise. The recording:

- is kept only in a temporary file while the card is open, and deleted when you move on, leave the page or quit (and, if Kikimi crashed, the next time it starts);
- is never uploaded, transcribed, analysed or included in a backup.

If you said no, the card says so and has a button to open **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone**. You can allow it there at any time. Read the [privacy policy](/kikimi/privacy/) for the full wording.
