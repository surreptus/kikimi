## What you can add

Video files on your Mac, which you own or may copy and study (see the [terms](/kikimi/terms/)). Kikimi never downloads anything from video sites, and your original file is only read, never changed or deleted. Audio-only files are not supported yet. Not sure where to find something? See [finding material](/kikimi/guide/finding-material/).

Add videos by dropping them on **Videos**, by pressing **Choose files**, or with <kbd>⌘O</kbd>. A video can be dropped together with its subtitle file.

## With subtitles

If you have an `.srt` or `.vtt` file for the video, add it and Kikimi uses its text and times instead of transcribing. A subtitle file with the same name as the video (such as `film.mp4` and `film.srt`) is paired with it automatically; otherwise press **Add subtitles** on the video's row. No speech model is needed, and it is the quickest way to start.

## Without subtitles: the speech model

Kikimi writes out what is said using a speech model that runs on your Mac. You download it once, from the Videos page:

| Language | Default model | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Spanish, French, German | Whisper medium | about 1.5 GB |
| Japanese | Whisper large-v3 turbo | 1.6 GB |

The model is saved on this Mac and nothing is uploaded. You can choose a different model for each language in **Settings > Languages**. Transcription accuracy depends on the model and the audio, and times can be a little off in fast, overlapping speech.

## What happens at each step

Each row shows the step it is on, such as *4 of 7 · Transcribing*.

1. **Reading file.** Checks you haven't added this video already.
2. **Copying.** Makes a working copy.
3. **Checking language.** Listens to a short sample, so a Japanese video isn't added as Spanish. If it sounds like a different language, Kikimi asks before going on.
4. **Transcribing.** The long step, and the longer the larger the model. Skipped with subtitles.
5. **Compressing video.** Saves a small copy for playback. In Settings you can choose **Original quality** to skip this.
6. **Verifying.** Checks the copy plays and is the right length.
7. **Saving.** Stores everything at once: all of it, or none of it.

You can cancel at any point before saving and nothing is left behind. You can use the other pages while an import runs.

## Where to find videos

Kikimi doesn't find videos for you. Some places publish video you may download and study, each under its own licence, such as the Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons. See the [home page](/kikimi/#finding-videos-to-learn-from).
