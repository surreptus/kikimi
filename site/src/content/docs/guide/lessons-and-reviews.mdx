The **Learn** page has three parts. It leads with the one to do first: reviews while any are due, otherwise lessons.

## Lessons

Each day Kikimi offers the new words you hear most in your videos, 15 a day by default. You can change that, or turn lessons off, in **Settings > Languages > New words a day**. Unused lessons lapse at the end of the day, and none are offered while more than 100 reviews are due, so words don't pile up.

A lesson teaches five words at a time, each with its meaning and a sentence from your own video, then quizzes those five straight away. A missed word is asked again, after the others, until it is right. The words join your reviews only when the quiz is finished, so leaving early starts nothing. **Already know it** and **Ignore** are there for words that don't belong.

## Reviews

A word you are learning moves through eight stages. Each time you get it right it comes back after a longer wait, and a miss brings it back sooner.

| Stage | Next review comes after |
|---|---|
| 1 | 4 hours |
| 2 | 8 hours |
| 3 | 1 day |
| 4 | 2 days |
| 5 | 1 week |
| 6 | 2 weeks |
| 7 | 1 month |
| 8 | 4 months |

After stage 8 a word is **Finished** and no longer comes back. **Got it** (<kbd>→</kbd>) moves a word up one stage. **Missed it** (<kbd>←</kbd>) moves it down one, never below stage 1. A word moves only on its first answer in a sitting; a missed word comes back later in the sitting without moving again. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to show the answer.

The number beside **Learn** in the sidebar is how many reviews are due now.

## Card types

Choose the kind of card above **Start**. It is used for reviews and practice, not for lessons, which always ask for the word and its meaning.

- **Meaning:** you see the word and recall its meaning.
- **Fill in:** a sentence from your videos with the word blanked, and a hint.
- **Sentence:** you hear a sentence from your videos, with its text hidden at first, and answer one of three ways:
  - **Listen:** hear it, then check what it said.
  - **Type:** type what you hear, and see what you missed.
  - **Shadow:** [say it aloud](/kikimi/guide/shadowing/) and compare yourself with the original.

A word with no sentence from your videos, or no short one for typing and shadowing, is asked as word and meaning.

## Practice

Practice moves nothing on your schedule, except that a known word you miss goes back to learning:

- **Review ahead:** words before they are due.
- **Practice finished:** words that have finished.
- **Review known:** a short quiz of words you marked known. It stops over-claiming from inflating a video's coverage.
