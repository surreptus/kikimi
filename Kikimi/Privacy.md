# Privacy policy

Kikimi is built so that your videos, and what you learn from them, stay on your Mac.

_Last updated: 27 October 2026._

## The short version

- Kikimi does not collect any information about you.
- Your videos, transcripts, words and progress never leave your Mac.
- The only time it uses the internet is when you ask it to download a speech model.
- There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your Mac

> Everything you add and learn is kept on your Mac, and only you can remove it.

Everything Kikimi works with is stored on your Mac, in the app's own folder:

- A copy of each video you add: smaller than the original by default, or exactly your file if you choose Original quality in Settings.
- The transcript of each video, and the words found in it.
- Which words you are learning, which you know, and your review history.
- A daily tally of your activity: how long you have watched, which words you looked up, and how many reviews you answered. It is only counts, kept to show your progress.
- The speech models you have downloaded.
- Your settings, such as the language you chose.

Your original video files are only ever read. Kikimi never changes or deletes them.

You can remove videos on the Videos page and speech models on the Storage page. Deleting the app's folder removes everything else.

## Transcription, dictionaries and speech

Transcription runs on your Mac using a speech model you have downloaded. The dictionaries used for word look-ups are built into the app. When Kikimi says a word aloud it uses the voices that come with macOS. None of this sends anything anywhere.

## The one thing that uses the internet

> The only time Kikimi goes online is to download a speech model, and it sends nothing else.

A few links open a web page in your browser, such as a Japanese grammar guide. That is your browser visiting the page, as when you click any link: Kikimi sends nothing, and the site sees your visit as any website would.

The first time you need a speech model, Kikimi downloads it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a service that hosts it. When it does, Hugging Face can see your internet address and which model files you asked for, as any website would, and handles that under its own privacy policy. Kikimi sends nothing else: not your videos, transcripts, words or how you use the app.

Once a model is downloaded, Kikimi works without an internet connection.

## Backups

If you choose Save backup in Settings, Kikimi writes a file with your progress to the place you pick. If you also choose to include your videos and transcripts, it writes a folder with them and a smaller copy of each video. It is never uploaded anywhere; where it goes after that is up to you.

## What Kikimi does not do

> Kikimi doesn't collect, track, advertise or share anything about you.

- It does not create accounts or ask for your name, email or any personal details.
- It does not track you or build a profile of you.
- It does not include advertising or sell or share data with anyone.
- It does not send analytics or crash reports of its own.

Your Mac and Apple may collect diagnostic information if you have chosen to share it in macOS settings. That is outside Kikimi's control.

## Children

Kikimi does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with the app and the date above will change.
